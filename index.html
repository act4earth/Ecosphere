Energy in cells: photosynthesis and cellular respiration

The two processes run in opposite directions. Each one makes what the other needs. Start with the big picture, then open the step-by-step animations or test yourself in the Label Labs.

Photosynthesis6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂
Cellular respirationC₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + ATP

How they compare

PhotosynthesisCellular respiration
WhereChloroplastCytoplasm, then mitochondrion
Which organismsPlants, algae and some bacteriaNearly all living things, plants included
ReactantsCO₂ + H₂O, using light energyGlucose + O₂
ProductsGlucose + O₂CO₂ + H₂O + ATP (and heat)
Energy changeLight energy → chemical energy in glucoseChemical energy in glucose → ATP
StagesLight-dependent reactions, Calvin cycleGlycolysis, link reaction, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain
Electron carrierNADPHNADH and FADH₂
H⁺ gradient builds inThylakoid spaceIntermembrane space
Final electron acceptorNADP⁺O₂
