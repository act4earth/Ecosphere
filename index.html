Climate change: the greenhouse effect and its consequences for life

Follow energy from the Sun to Earth and back out to space, see how greenhouse gases and the carbon cycle change that balance, then explore what warming does to ecosystems and how we can respond. Each topic has step-by-step explanations and an interactive model.
